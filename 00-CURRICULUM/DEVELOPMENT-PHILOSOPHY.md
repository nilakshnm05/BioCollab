# BioCollab Development Philosophy

## Core Principle

Learn while building.

We do not separate:
    Learning → Project

Instead:

    Learn concept
         ↓
    Understand WHY
         ↓
    Apply to BioCollab
         ↓
    Debug / Review
         ↓
    Document
         ↓
    Commit

---

## Engineering Mindset

Before writing code:

1. What problem are we solving?
2. Who should own this responsibility?
3. What data is involved?
4. What component should handle it?
5. What happens when the data changes?
6. What happens when the data is empty or invalid?

---

## Quality Principles

- Understand before copying.
- Prefer simple solutions over unnecessary complexity.
- Build reusable components.
- Keep responsibilities separated.
- Prefer data-driven UI.
- Handle empty/error/loading states.
- Use stable identities for dynamic data.
- Think about accessibility and performance early.
- Review before committing.

---

## AI Usage

AI should accelerate engineering, not replace understanding.

Preferred workflow:

AI explains
    ↓
I understand
    ↓
I implement
    ↓
I test
    ↓
I review

Never:

AI writes
    ↓
Blindly ship
